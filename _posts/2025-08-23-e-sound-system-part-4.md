The e-Sound system is working fine, but I still had to intall the speaker in
the front bumper area. I figured that it should be mounted close to the front
right wheel, and according to ETKA it needs two brackets, `5GE 035 205` and
`5GE 035 369`. I already received these, some time ago. The information still
lacking was how to mount the brackets. Base on information from Elsawin, I
figured that the holes of one of the brackets should probably be aligned with
the bumper bar mounting holes.

{% responsive_image path: "assets/posts/2025-08-23/elsawin.jpeg" alt: "My initial guess on how to mount the bracket (but it should be on the bottom side)." %}

I asked another friend with an e-Golf and a factory-fitted e-Sound system to
see if he could spot the bracket. Unfortunately, he could not see it from
above. So the only way to find out how was to remove the bumper.

Another friend of mine helped me to remove the bumper carefully. He has enough
experience, so it was done without any damage. There are plenty of tutorials
on YouTube on how to do this, but my advice is to do this with at least two
persons. We also found out that the bumper had been removed before, because
two screws below the grill were missing.

{% responsive_image path: "assets/posts/2025-08-23/bumper-removed.jpeg" alt: "The bumper removed." %}

We could then see how the bracket should be mounted, and it was indeed on the
bumper bar, but on the bottom side. Removing the bottom two bolts was
sufficient, and ensures that the car does not need to be aligned afterwards.
The second bracket with the speaker already mounted was then mounted onto
the first bracket

{% responsive_image path: "assets/posts/2025-08-23/bumper-bar.jpeg" alt: "The bracket lines up with the bumper bar." %}

The mounting bracket for the horn was in the way, and I probably needed a third
bracket to mount this to the speaker bracket. Since I did not have this
bracket, we decided created one from an angle bracket of suffiecient strength.
This will suffice for now, and I might replace it someday. I hope I can access
it by removing the bottom plate only.

{% responsive_image path: "assets/posts/2025-08-23/installed.jpeg" alt: "The speaker mounted on its proper location." %}

From the photo you can see the yellow wire loop. Until today, I mounted the
speaker somewhere close to the ABS pump with a few tie wraps. Because I already
prepared the wiring, I simply extended it. I can now remove that wire loop,
and crimp the connector one more time. I should be able to do this from within
the wheel arch, where I can simply remove the cover.

Some people have asked me why I would want to install the e-Sound system. After
all, the benefit of electric cars is that they are silent. As mentioned in the
first part, pedestrian warning systems are mandatory in the EU for all new
electric cars, so sooner or later all cars will have it. I do believe they
improve safety as well, and to be honest: it does not sound bad.
